    How the picks are made

    The match odds and the over/under 2.5 goals odds are converted to probabilities, with the bookie's margin removed (power method, which corrects for longshots being overpriced).

    The model then finds each team's expected goals that reproduce those probabilities, using a Dixon-Coles model. That is a Poisson goals model with a correction for 0-0, 1-0, 0-1 and 1-1, which plain Poisson gets wrong. The result is a probability for every scoreline.

    In 6 Scores mode the pick is simply the most likely exact score in each game. The games are independent, so the chance of all six coming in is the six chances multiplied together, and that is highest when each pick is the most likely score. Tap another score to swap to it; the card shows how much of your jackpot chance the swap costs. A swap can make sense when the alternative is close in probability but likely picked by fewer people, because the jackpot is shared between winners.

    Shuffle builds a random ticket weighted by the odds. Every score from 0-0 up to 5-2 can come up, including underdog wins, roughly as often as the odds say it happens. The randomness slider sharpens that towards the likeliest scores or flattens it towards pure chance. "Underdog wins per shuffle" forces at least that many upset results, choosing the games where an upset is most plausible.

    "Avoid popular scores" penalises the scores most players are likely to pick: 2-1, 1-1, 2-0 and 1-0 to the favourite. Bet365 doesn't publish what players pick, so these popularity figures are an estimate, not data. It applies to both Best picks and Shuffle. It doesn't make a win more likely; it makes a win less likely to be shared.

    The repeat limit caps how many times one score can appear across your six picks. When the limit bites, the app makes the swaps that cost the least overall jackpot chance, and the summary shows how much of the best possible chance you keep.

    In Points league mode the pick is the score with the highest expected points: correct-score points × P(exact score) + result points × P(right result, wrong score). This favours the likeliest score inside the likeliest result, and in one-sided games it moves to 2-0 or 3-0 on its own.

    Without over/under odds the model assumes a typical draw adjustment and fits goals from the match odds alone. It still works, but the goals total is less well pinned down.

    Odds can be typed as 11/4, 6-4, evs or 3.75. A bare number like 2 is read as decimal odds.

    The fixture list comes from The Odds API and is refreshed on Tuesday morning, Thursday and Friday evening, and Saturday morning. Each price is the middle (median) price across UK bookmakers, which is at least as accurate for the model as one bookie's price. Fixtures you've added pick up new prices automatically when the list refreshes.
